## Staircase gates

### S0 — Foundation sealed

Persistent state and action ledger are authoritative outside chat.

### S1 — Data sealed

Canonical XSMB representation is stable. FULL_PRIZE provenance and LEGACY_TAIL27 are never conflated.

### S2 — Evidence sealed

Compact evidence envelopes preserve source→candidate lineage and hashes.

### S3 — Runtime sealed

Brain and data/runtime services communicate only through registered corridors.

### S4 — Governance sealed

Default-deny capability gates, replay protection and fail-closed behavior are runtime verified.

### S5 — Render sealed

The deployed runtime is verified without triggering destructive rebuilds or hidden heavy computation.

### S6 — Staircase opened

Only after S0–S5 pass may L1 be unlocked.

## Layer 1

Layer 1 may contain intelligence/research/Quant logic, but it remains subordinate to foundation governance.

No L1 component may:

- rewrite source truth;
- bypass evidence gates;
- access protected rooms without a corridor;
- mint its own capability;
- change OOS boundaries;
- silently rebuild manifests;
- convert failed evidence into promotion.
