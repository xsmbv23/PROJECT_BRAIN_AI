## Decision

There is exactly **ONE Forensic FSM**.

Database existence, binding, secret resolution, TLS admission, network-origin proof, durable round-trip, and promotion are ordered gates/evidence states within that one FSM.

## Critical rule

`PASS_IS_LOCAL` and `NO_PASS_INHERITANCE` are mandatory.

A PASS only proves its own gate. It permits evaluation of the next gate; it never sets the next gate to PASS.

FAIL/UNKNOWN stops downstream reachability. Downstream states are `UNREACHED`, not PASS.
