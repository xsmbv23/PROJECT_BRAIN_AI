# BRAIN-N045 — Brain Governance Privilege Boundary

## Finding

Review of N044 exposed a real architectural gap: Brain checked a capability identifier but did not require the corresponding scoped, expiring `CapabilityLease`. That left a future implementation path where a caller could present a syntactically valid capability ID without proving corridor scope, nonce, expiry, operation limit, or destination-layer scope.

## Corrective action

`core/brain_role.py` now requires a `CapabilityLease` for every Brain admission.

The lease is verified for:

- corridor identity;
- capability identity;
- nonce / replay protection;
- policy version;
- expiry;
- operation count;
- source-layer scope;
- destination-layer scope.

Brain still rejects itself as a destination (`BRAIN_SELF_SERVICE_DENY`).

## New invariant

```text
Brain admission
    requires
scoped + expiring capability lease
    AND
policy pin
    AND
schema-major compatibility
    AND
layer-direction compatibility
```

Therefore:

```text
Brain PASS
= ADMISSIBLE_FOR_NEXT_GATE
```

and never:

```text
Brain PASS = ENTRY
Brain PASS = TRADE
Brain PASS = DATA_TRUSTED
Brain PASS = CALCULATION_VALID
```

## Test expansion

`tests/test_brain_role.py` now covers expiry, corridor mismatch, nonce, destination-layer mismatch, policy mismatch, schema-major mismatch, self-service denial and the non-final nature of Brain admission.

## Fosennic interpretation

Brain is a guarded corridor dispatcher, not the owner of the rooms. Every downstream room retains its own lock and final authority.

## OOM

No dataset or network loading was introduced. The Brain layer remains metadata-only and compatible with the Render Free 512MB / 1 CPU boundary.
