## Identity doctrine

Source identity is part of chain of custody.

The following are distinct and may not be inferred from one another:

```text
requested host
redirect chain
final host
canonical source identity
source independence
capture eligibility
```

A redirect is evidence of a redirect, not proof that the requested source and final host are the same canonical source.

A different hostname is not proof of source independence.

Advertising and advertising redirects are non-truth content and cannot establish source identity.
