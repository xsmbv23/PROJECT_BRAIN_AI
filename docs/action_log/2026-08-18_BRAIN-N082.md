## Why autonomous execution stops here

The service is bound to a TLS-capable PostgreSQL configuration, but the exact runtime probe cannot resolve its database host from Oregon. The system must not manufacture an external infrastructure event.

The required external event is one of:

1. Replace the Render Secret `DATABASE_URL` with a credential-valid externally reachable PostgreSQL endpoint whose DNS is resolvable from the Oregon service and whose TLS admission is provable; or
2. Move the database/service topology to a same-region arrangement and re-bind the secret without losing evidence/data authority.

The current control surface does not expose the secret value, so the Brain cannot safely construct or replace the credential-bearing URL itself.
