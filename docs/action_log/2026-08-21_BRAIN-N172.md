## Non-negotiable invariant

The acquisition mechanism is transport. It is not trust.

Every channel must provide:

- real provenance;
- acquisition timestamp;
- exact raw bytes;
- raw-byte SHA-256;
- raw byte count > 0;
- coverage exactly 1.0;
- zero unresolved conflicts;
- `synthetic_data=false`.

Failure is DENY.
