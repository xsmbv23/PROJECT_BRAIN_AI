# BRAIN-N044 — Brain Role in the Foundation

## Architectural decision

Brain is the **governance/control plane**, not the data plane, calculation engine, or UI.

```text
CHAT/UI
   │
   │ communication only
   ▼
BRAIN GOVERNANCE PLANE
   │
   ├─ policy pin
   ├─ schema-major gate
   ├─ capability gate
   ├─ layer-edge gate
   ├─ security-chain gate
   ├─ quarantine / circuit-breaker decisions
   └─ durable audit metadata
   │
   ├───────────────┐
   ▼               ▼
DATA PLANE      ENGINE PLANE
(source truth)  (calculation/algorithms)
```

## Brain must NOT own

- raw XSMB datasets;
- FULL_27 / TAIL_27 source data;
- market calculations;
- Quant algorithms;
- database credentials;
- scraper credentials;
- Render operational secrets;
- the application UI;
- a master access key;
- authority to bypass downstream room/layer gates.

The existing hardening primitives already define policy pins, capability leases, quarantine, circuit breaking, schema-major validation and tamper-evident audit metadata. `brain_role.py` now makes the plane boundary executable rather than merely documentary.

## Brain may own

- immutable governance policy references;
- state/version compatibility checks;
- capability admission decisions;
- routing/admissibility decisions;
- security-chain orchestration;
- quarantine decisions;
- compact audit/provenance metadata;
- successor-Bot state and next-action instructions.

## Critical invariant

```text
Brain can DENY.
Brain can ADMIT a request to the NEXT gate.
Brain cannot manufacture final authority for another plane.
```

Therefore a Brain PASS means only:

`ADMISSIBLE_FOR_NEXT_GATE`

and never means `ENTRY`, `TRADE`, `DATA_TRUSTED`, or `CALCULATION_VALID`.

## OOM policy

Brain remains metadata-only. Large datasets and expensive calculations stay outside Brain runtime. This preserves the Render Free 512MB boundary.

## Promotion

DENY. Layer 1 LOCKED. Staircase LOCKED until verifier evidence and foundation closure audit pass.
