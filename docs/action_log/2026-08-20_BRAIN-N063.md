# BRAIN-N063 — Forensic Succession Boundary

## Objective

Persist the architectural rule requested for successor Bots: the system must transmit verification capability and immutable invariants, not inherited trust.

## Decision

Formalized the five-way separation:

```text
DOCTRINE != EVIDENCE != STATE != HISTORY != HYPOTHESIS
```

Added the critical hypothesis boundary:

```text
HYPOTHESIS != EDGE
EDGE != EV+
```

A Quant hypothesis must pass controlled test, replay, OOS validation, stability evaluation, edge promotion, probability assessment, and payout/cost analysis before EV is considered.

If EV < 0, production promotion is DENY regardless of historical enthusiasm or hypothesis quality.

## Succession rule

> A successor Bot inherits the right and duty to verify; it does not inherit the right to trust.

## Fossilization guard

Historical implementations, websites, parsers, deployment IDs, database implementations, sensors, old hypotheses, and old thresholds are not frozen as eternal truth. They may be replaced when evidence justifies a safer/correct implementation.

The invariants remain versioned doctrine unless deliberately revised through a documented, tested doctrine change.
